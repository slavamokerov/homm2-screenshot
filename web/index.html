Builds a poster PNG from a Heroes of Might and Magic II save: the adventure map (with the fog of war of one player or without it), castles, hero cards and a row of info panels — minimap, resources, events, rumors, the obelisk puzzle and the win/loss conditions. fheroes2 saves are supported as well. Everything runs in the browser: the save and the game data are read from your disk.

Download PNG (300 DPI)

Print at 100% (disable “fit to page”). The PNG stores 300 DPI, so the physical size is exact.

Where the game data is

Pick the folder that contains HEROES2.AGG (HEROES2X.AGG for The Price of Loyalty). The page scans the selected folder recursively, so saves in subfolders are found too.

  • HoMM2 (GOG, Windows): C:\GOG Games\HoMM2 Gold
  • HoMM2 (GOG Galaxy, Windows): C:\Program Files (x86)\GOG Galaxy\Games\Heroes of Might and Magic 2 Gold
  • HoMM2 (CD, Windows): C:\HEROES2
  • HoMM2 (DOSBox): the folder mounted as C: (e.g. C:\DOSGAMES\HEROES2); saves are in the GAMES subfolder next to HEROES2.AGG
  • HoMM2 (GOG, macOS): /Applications/Heroes of Might and Magic II.app/Contents/Resources
  • HoMM2 (Wine/Proton, Linux): ~/.wine/drive_c/HEROES2
  • fheroes2 (Windows): %APPDATA%\fheroes2
  • fheroes2 (macOS): ~/Library/Application Support/fheroes2
  • fheroes2 (Linux): ~/.local/share/fheroes2
  • fheroes2 (Flatpak): ~/.var/app/io.github.ihhub.Fheroes2/data/fheroes2
